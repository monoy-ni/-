# 冬天起床：日式漫画

用途：产品功能设计案例中的局部漫画。按用户要求混合照片、记录、结构图和漫画；本轮仅新增这张漫画，开发部分保持现状。具体画面待用户审阅。

方式：内置 image_gen 生图工具。图片：[winter-getting-up-manga-v1.png](../../assets/comics/winter-getting-up-manga-v1.png)。结尾为“待观察”，未预设验证成功。

## 最终提示词

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Make ONE beautiful Japanese-style educational manga for a Chinese beginner course, portrait approximately 1536 × 2048. FOUR wide narrative comic panels stacked vertically, read top to bottom, white gutters, no separate page title. Clean confident black ink, soft natural colors, winter morning light, expressive everyday campus manga, refined and friendly, not a UI diagram, not an infographic and not chibi. Accurate large SIMPLIFIED CHINESE lettering, oversized speech balloons with very short dialogue so it remains legible on a phone. No Japanese lettering, no watermark, no brand logos.
Protagonist 小明 is an adult college student, short dark slightly messy hair, round glasses, simple blue long-sleeve pajamas, consistently drawn in all panels. He is under a thick blue quilt in an ordinary modest student bedroom on a cold winter morning. Snow outside the window. Clothes and school bag nearby establish that he needs to get up for school. Show normal everyday indoor scenes, no bare body.
Panel 1: Xiaoming huddles under the cozy quilt, only his head and a hand visible, looking toward the chilly room. His exact balloon: “太冷了，不想起床……”
Panel 2: Close-medium scene: he looks at his phone. A suggestion comes from the PHONE, with a balloon tail toward the phone: “那做个网课平台？” Xiaoming looks puzzled, responding in his OWN balloon: “我只是觉得冷……” A small simple imagined laptop screen can show the suggestion, without extra lettering or extra person.
Panel 3: He considers a wall-mounted air conditioner/heater and its remote. Show a thoughtful imagined warm room with subtle warm airflow, as a proposed change, not a proven result. Exact thought balloon: “先把房间暖起来？”
Panel 4: He is still preparing the trial, not celebrating a result or already at school. Sitting wrapped in the quilt with a notebook and pen, ready to make an observation. Exact balloon: “先试一次，看看效果。” The notebook has one large short question, exact: “更愿意起床吗？” Add a small clear note “待观察”. Do NOT show a completed test, a claimed improvement, temperature numbers, a measured result, a ticked success box or a promise that heating solves everything.
Meaning: identify the actual obstacle before inventing unrelated features; choose a small change and observe its effect. Let acting, environment and sequential scenes carry this meaning. No explanatory paragraphs. The FOUR panels should be handsome, spacious and distinctly drawn scenes with natural framing changes. Keep all specified Chinese text accurate and readable.
```
